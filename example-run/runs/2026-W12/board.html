What the board answers

Which installed accounts would likely churn without an escalation, and why. Every account gets one status each week, and every reason links to the call or note it comes from.

The four statuses

Red
Would likely churn without escalation. Kept rare: one explicit churn signal from the customer, or two strong warning signs that have lasted 3 weeks or more.
Yellow
Something is off, not at escalation level: one warning sign, a negative trend younger than 3 weeks, or an open ask nobody has answered.
Green
Engaged, tone normal or positive, no serious open complaint. Needs at least one customer call in the window.
No signal
No customer call in the last 4 weeks. Internal notes add context but cannot make an account green, because silence is not health.

What never counts against an account

  • A missing recording with no explanation. It is marked unknown, never a no-show.
  • Internal calls. They explain context but are not the customer's voice.
  • Deliberate product design the customer agreed to.

Reasons are written about the account, never about the account owner.
